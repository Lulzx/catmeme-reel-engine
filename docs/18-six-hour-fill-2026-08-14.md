six-hour YouTube grid from August 18 through August 31, 2026. It records the
authoring, editorial gates, casting, rendering, upload state, and remote schedule.

## Authoring decisions

- 56 six-beat stories: four releases per day for fourteen days.
- Office/digital, commute, food, household, and family/friend lanes are interleaved.
- Consecutive releases never reuse the same scene.
- Every story uses role labels only; no personal names are present.
- Clips 182 and 183 retain their recurring speaking roles, while other clips are
  globally allocated for freshness.
- Every final beat is a physical consequence, callback, reversal, or specific
  escalation; no generic defeat ending was accepted.
